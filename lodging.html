Lodging

Find a place to stay

Taniti offers accommodations across a range of budgets and travel styles.

  • Hostel
    An inexpensive option for budget-minded travelers.
  • Small hotels
    Many are family-owned.
  • Bed and breakfasts
    A growing lodging category on the island.
  • Four-star resort
    Taniti has one large resort.

All types of lodging are strictly regulated and regularly inspected by the Tanitian government.
