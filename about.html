About the island

Meet Taniti

Taniti is a small tropical island in the Pacific with less than 500 square miles of varied terrain.

The island includes sandy and rocky beaches, a small safe harbor, lush tropical rainforests, and a mountainous interior with a small active volcano. Taniti has an indigenous population of about 20,000. Fishing and agriculture historically dominated the economy before the recent increase in tourism.

Visitor focus: Taniti City, Yellow Leaf Bay, Merriton Landing, the beaches, rainforest, and volcano are central to many tourist experiences.
