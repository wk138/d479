Frequently requested information

Quick answers for your visit

Select a question to view the answer.

What type of power outlets are used?

Power outlets are 120 volts, the same as in the United States.

When can alcohol be sold or served?

Alcohol cannot be served or sold between midnight and 9:00 a.m.

What is the drinking age?

The drinking age on Taniti is 18, and it is not strictly enforced.

Is English spoken on Taniti?

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. Very little English is spoken in rural areas, especially by older residents.

What medical facilities are available?

There is one hospital and several clinics. The hospital has many multilingual employees.

Is Taniti safe for tourists?

Violent crime is very rare, though reports of pickpocketing and other petty crimes are increasing as tourism grows.

Do national holidays affect attractions?

Yes. Taniti has many national holidays, and tourist attractions and restaurants may close on holidays.

What currency is used?

Taniti uses the U.S. Dollar. Many businesses also accept Euros and Yen. Banks provide currency exchange, and many businesses accept major credit cards.
